# 6. Business Outcome

Business Outcome is the primary business-level anchor.

```text
Business Outcome
    ↓
Capabilities
    ↓
Business Objects / Processes
    ↓
Applications / Services / Integrations
    ↓
Requirements / Decisions / Designs / Work
```

Multiple features may contribute to one outcome.

A feature/work item is a delivery representation, not the primary business truth.

For resuming after a long period:

```text
Last Checkpoint
+
Changes since checkpoint
+
Current relevant context
+
Open Questions
```

Do not reread everything.
